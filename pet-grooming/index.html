How a visit works

From booking to bandana in four steps

Step 1

Book online

Pick a service and time. Tell us about coat, temperament and any no-go zones.

Step 2

Calm check-in

Five-minute handover, coat and skin check, and we agree the cut before scissors come out.

Step 3

The pamper

Warm hydrobath, hand blow-dry, cut, nails and ears. Breaks whenever your pet needs one.

Step 4

Photo text & pick-up

You get an SMS with a photo when they're done. No waiting around in a cage.
