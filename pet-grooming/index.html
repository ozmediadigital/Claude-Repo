Questions owners ask

Can't see yours? Ask when you book and we'll text you back.

How long will my pet be with you?

Bath and tidy takes 60–90 minutes and a full groom takes 2–3 hours, depending on size and coat. We text you a photo when they're ready, so you're never waiting around.

My dog is anxious. Can you still groom them?

Yes. We book anxious dogs into quiet first-of-the-day slots, take breaks whenever needed and stop if your pet is too stressed. You only pay for what we finish.

What if my dog's coat is matted?

We check the coat at drop-off. Light matting is brushed out at $15 per 15 minutes. If it's severe, we'll recommend a short clip-down, which is kinder than hours of brushing, and agree the price with you first.

Do you need vaccination records?

We ask for current C3 or C5 vaccinations for dogs and F3 for cats. Puppies can come for an intro visit after their second vaccination.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation or rebooking up to 24 hours before. If your pet is unwell on the day, rebook for free.
